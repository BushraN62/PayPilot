How this estimate is calculated

Estimated cost equals four years of annual tuition plus median student debt. Estimated return uses ten years of median annual earnings.

ROI = ((10-year earnings − estimated cost) ÷ estimated cost) × 100

This comparison does not include scholarships, living expenses, taxes, interest, wage growth, or earnings that may have occurred without attending college.
